## First 3D Platform Game Ever

Alpha-Waves was the first 3D platform game ever created. I started working on it in 1988, and it was published by Infogrames in 1990.

### Gameplay

A puzzle/labyrinth game where you jumped from platform to platform to reach doors giving access to the next room.

### Innovation

In my opinion, the first truly immersive 3D game, combining:
- True 6-axis 3D movement
- Physics engine (primitive but effective)
- Interaction with a large number of objects
- Fully 3D environment

### Genesis & Technology

Its genesis, technology, and references to games of the same era are all documented on my blog. I published the source code for archeologists interested in obsolete technologies.

### Recognition

Tamaracade published a really good video on the topic, and it's documented on Wikipedia as a milestone in gaming history.

### Modern Port

At some point between jobs, I rewrote Alpha Waves in C to play it on modern machines. The new job caught up before I could finish the port, but the project is available on SourceForge.
