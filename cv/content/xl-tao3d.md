## Extensible languages designed to evolve

- XL — an extensible language designed to evolve (from earlier LX work)
- Tao3D — XL derivative for interactive 3D graphics and presentations
- ELFE — experiment for single-source programming in the Internet of Things
- Advanced generics and a simplified parse tree across implementations
- XLR / Tao3D lineage for dynamic 3D animations
