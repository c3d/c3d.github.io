## A 32-Year Writing Journey

Informagie is a novel I started writing as a student, around the same time I was developing Alpha Waves. I finished it after 32 years and published it.

### Story

A humorous science-fiction auto-biography by Antoine Ravelin, explaining how a video game saves humanity with the help of dinosaurs.

### Features

- Unionized green mice
- A robotic vacuum cleaner that can tell you if the woman of your life is a cyborg
- A psychopath antivirus gone allergic
- A chlorophyl-based Internet where spam occupies the vast majority of Canada's forests

### Language

Written in French. Working on an English translation, tentatively named "Nin, Binary," but this is super-low priority in the grand scheme of things.

### Philosophy

This kind of thing makes me laugh. I hope it will make you laugh too.
