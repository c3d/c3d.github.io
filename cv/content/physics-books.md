## Unifying General Relativity and Quantum Mechanics

As an amateur physicist, I've long been very interested in quantum mechanics and relativity.

### Theory of Incomplete Measurements

A long-sought and improbable solution to the problem of unifying quantum mechanics and general relativity.

**Core Principle:** A new principle of relativity suggesting we should be able to do physics using any instrument, including a broken voltmeter or watching through foggy glasses.

**New Mathematics:** We must learn a new way to do physics:
- Without equations or real numbers
- No continuum, no space, no time
- Only discrete measurements of space and time
- Measurements not all equivalent to one another
- New changes of reference frame for non-linear or irreversible conversions

**Probavectors:** Digital pictures record the probability of detecting photons and predict the probability for a screen to emit them—the functional equivalent of a wave function, but in discrete form.
