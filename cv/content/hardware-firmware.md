## Low-level platform concepts

- Emulated PCI/PCIe configuration, MMIO, DMA and interrupts, UEFI and ACPI, a virtual BMC and Itanium MCA in HPVM
- Device drivers for VXI, VME and SCPI instruments (RTICL), plus Linux and RTOS drivers (DE200C, DxO ONE, DB48x)
- Secure Boot, TPM, NUMA topologies and power management
