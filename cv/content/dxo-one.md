## Ultra-Compact High-Quality Camera

Designed the firmware and software quality for a highly capable connected camera at DxO Labs in Paris.

### Technical Challenge

The DxO ONE was a dual-core ARM + 88-bit real-time system, combining:
- High-end image sensor and optics
- Real-time image processing
- iOS connectivity
- Compact form factor

### Software Architecture

Responsible for:
- Firmware quality and architecture
- Real-time image processing pipeline
- iOS app integration
- System-level optimization

### Innovation

Ultra-compact camera that could attach directly to iPhone while maintaining DSLR-level image quality, thanks to DxO's renowned image processing expertise.
