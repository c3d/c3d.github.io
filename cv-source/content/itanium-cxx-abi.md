## Common ABI for representing C++ programs

- After exception-handling work, proposed a common C++ ABI for Itanium with Cary Coutant
- Met with C++ engineers from many companies (with Daveed Vandevoorde)
- Result: a standard-compliant C++ ABI for data and program representation on Itanium
- Adopted by other platforms starting with GCC (Mark Mitchell as primary maintainer of the documentation)
- Still called the Itanium C++ ABI; adapted on many platforms and maintained for new C++ revisions
