## Home entertainment center from an HP Linux appliance

- HP DE200C digital entertainment appliance (Linux PC in a CD-player shell)
- Converted with MythTV, DVD drive, more RAM, and a TV tuner
- Wrote a client/server driver for the front-panel VFD display
- Linux patches for remote control and front-panel keys
