## First 3D platformer ever

- Started in 1988, published by Infogrames in 1990
- First 3D platform game; true 6-axis 3D with physics-like interaction
- Ran on Atari ST, Amiga, and 80286 PCs
- Influenced Frederick Raynal (Alone in the Dark)
- Source later published for historians of obsolete technology

**Quote:** *"Playing the game was a surreal experience..."* — Frederick Raynal
