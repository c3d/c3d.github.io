## Language for interactive 3D

- Programming language for interactive 3D, derived from XLR
- Makes visualisation and animations quick to create; suited to talks, trade shows, museums, and education
- Commercial version from Taodyne (co-founded) adds stereoscopy, auto-stereoscopy, encrypted documents, and related features
- Used for Breakout demo, Thales sonar serious game, Firefly, and 3DIVART work
