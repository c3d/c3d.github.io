## Multi-equation numerical solver on Atari ST

- MuSolver was a quasi-clone of TK!Solver for the Atari ST
- First graphical app — TK!Solver-style system of non-linear equations
- Added complex numbers and stronger graphing vs TK!Solver
- Written in GFA BASIC; interpreter worked, buggy compiler blocked shipping
