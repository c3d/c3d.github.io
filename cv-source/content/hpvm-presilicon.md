## HPVM on [Ski](https://github.com/linux-ia64/ski/tree/hp-sim-for-linux), then early silicon

- Early HPVM (HP Integrity VM) boot was done entirely on [Ski](https://github.com/linux-ia64/ski/tree/hp-sim-for-linux), HP’s functional Itanium simulator — physical hardware took a while to arrive
- First silicon was an A-step (A-series) chip with a few functional defects; those were fixed in later tapeouts
