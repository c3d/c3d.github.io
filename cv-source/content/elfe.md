## Extensible Language for Everyday (and the Internet of Things)

- Small general-purpose language tuned for configuration and control of swarms of sensors and actuators
- Can serve as a remotely accessible extension language for larger applications
- Trimmed-down XL that runs without LLVM
- First release to incorporate a tree-shape type system designed in late 2012
- Formerly called ELIoT (Extensible Language for the Internet of Things); renamed after a trademark complaint
