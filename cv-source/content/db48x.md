## A Modern Implementation of Reverse Polish Lisp

The DB48X project intends to rebuild and improve the user experience of the HP48 family of calculators, notably their "Reverse Polish Lisp" (RPL) language with its rich set of data types and built-in functions, and Lisp-level programming power.

### Target Platforms

The project presently targets the SwissMicro DM42 and DM32 calculators, leveraging their built-in software platform known as DMCP. There is also a simulator tested on macOS and Linux.

### Long-term Vision

Port DB48X to various physical calculator platforms:
- ARM-based HP50 and related machines (HP49, HP48Gii)
- HP Prime (at least the G1 model)
- Other calculator platforms

### Presentation

This project was presented at FOSDEM in 2023 and an update was given in 2024, showcasing the modern approach to calculator programming.

### Heritage

Building on decades of calculator programming experience, from the original HPDS development system to this modern reimplementation of RPL.
